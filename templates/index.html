30 January 2024: Pehli Mulakat Ka Wo Pal
Wo Raaste Aur Wo Bheed: Ghar me chote bhai ke function ki raunaq thi, sab log aane-jaane me vyast the. Par us din ka sabse khaas moment tab aaya jab aapne kadam rakha.
Wo White Suit Ka Jaadu: Aapne white suit pehna hua tha—itna saadgi bhara look ki apko dekhte hi baaki sab dhundhla ho gaya.
Wo Takraana Aur Darr: Achanak aap mughse takra gayi! Us ek pal ke liye waqt ruk gaya. aapki aankhon me ek halka sa darr aur sharm aa gayi, jaise unhe samajh hi na aaya ho ki kya hua.
Dil Ki Dhadkan: Aapko itne paas se dekhkar aur wo innocent sa darr dekhkar mere andar bas 'dhak-dhak' hone laga. Mai tumhare is pyaare se roop ko dekh kar wahin pyaar me kho gaye.
Ek Na Bhoolne Wali Yaad: Maine khud kya pehna tha, aas-paas kaun tha... kuch yaad nahi raha. Yaad raha toh bas 30 January ka wo din, wo white suit, aapka takraana aur wo pehli mulakat..
